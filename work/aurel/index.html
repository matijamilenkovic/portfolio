AUREL Dental Studio

A calm bilingual multi-page experience for a fictional premium dental studio, designed to make complex care feel clear, credible and human.

Clarity builds trust.

AUREL explores how a healthcare website can reduce uncertainty before the first visit. The experience connects services, the clinical team, patient guidance and a demonstration booking flow through calm language and a clear information hierarchy.

Deep teal, cool white and precise geometric surfaces create a clinical but approachable identity. A system-grotesk type stack, square geometry and consistent action patterns keep the Serbian and English journeys easy to scan across desktop and mobile.

Every screen answers the same question: what happens next?

Behind the design

Goal

Help visitors understand treatments, meet the team and find a clear route to booking. This concept is not a real clinic or medical service.

Design decisions

Separate service, team and advice pages reduce information density. A consistent booking action connects those pages.

Implementation

A bilingual interface, mobile navigation and step-by-step booking demonstration. Demo data is not sent to a clinic.

Scope: self-initiated concept. No client results, conversion figures or user research outcomes are claimed. The demo illustrates design and frontend implementation.

Visual system

A compact system of color and typography gives every screen a recognizable voice.
