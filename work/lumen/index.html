LUMEN Coffee & Kitchen

A warm, cinematic website for a fictional coffee and kitchen concept, designed around slow moments, strong typography and tactile hospitality.

Designed to slow time.

LUMEN explores how a restaurant website can communicate atmosphere before information. The experience gives imagery and typography room to breathe, then introduces the menu, reservations and practical details without breaking the mood.

A restrained cream, ink and caramel system creates warmth without becoming nostalgic. Editorial scale changes and gentle movement establish rhythm, while the menu and reservation flows remain direct and usable across devices.

The interface behaves like the space itself: warm, composed and never in a hurry.

Behind the design

Goal

Communicate the café atmosphere while making the menu, location and reservations easy to find. The brand is fictional.

Design decisions

A large photograph establishes the atmosphere; separate sections explain the menu and space. Menu and booking actions remain explicitly labelled.

Implementation

Static HTML with an interactive menu and reservation demo. The portfolio preview uses a still homepage image rather than an embedded animated website.

Scope: self-initiated concept. No client results, conversion figures or user research outcomes are claimed. The demo illustrates design and frontend implementation.

Visual system

A compact system of color and typography gives every screen a recognizable voice.
